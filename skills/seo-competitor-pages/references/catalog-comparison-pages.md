# Catalog Comparison Pages (item vs item, both sold by you)

For two products or product types you both sell (for example "[Type A] vs
[Type B]"), as opposed to your product vs a competitor's.

- URL pattern: `/compare/[a]-vs-[b]`. Pick pairs from search demand, the
  question bank and AI prompt-panel gaps.
- H1: "[A] vs [B]: key differences" (plus an audience qualifier if useful).
- Answer-first summary (50-70 words): which one suits which need.
- Side-by-side table: specs, how each works, evidence, price range, best for.
- One section per item, with cited evidence for each.
- Use-case matrix: "choose A if ..., choose B if ...".
- 5-7 comparison-specific questions answered on the page.
- Links to both product pages and related guides; both product pages link back.
- Put a short version of the table on each product page too
  (`seo-ecommerce/references/product-depth-and-linking.md`).
- Schema: Article (plus ItemList of the compared products if useful).
- Stay neutral and verifiable; no unsupported superiority claims.
