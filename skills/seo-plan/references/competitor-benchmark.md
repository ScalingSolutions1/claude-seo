# Competitor Benchmark (structure for COMPETITOR-ANALYSIS.md)

## Choose competitors

Use the user's list, or the 2-3 domains that most often share the top 10 for
the target keywords. Search competitors can differ from business competitors;
include both, labelled.

## Per competitor, observe

Indexed content URLs (sitemap count), publishing frequency (dates over the last
6-12 months), formats (guides, listicles, tools, templates, video, data
studies), depth, topics covered, structured data, internal-linking pattern,
and programmatic or templated page sets.

## Then list

- **Keyword overlap:** where each competitor outranks you.
- **Keyword gaps:** keywords where they rank and you don't.
- **Blue ocean:** topics no competitor covers well.
- **Exploitable weaknesses:** outdated, thin or inaccurate competitor pages that rank.
- **Link-worthy assets:** competitor pages that earned the most links.
- **Beatable competitors:** low-authority sites ranking top 3; copy their page
  type and outdo them on depth and internal links.
- **Your advantages** to lean into.

## Rules

- Base every claim on something observed and cite the URL; tool numbers carry
  source and date.
- Recommend actions, not just differences: Action | Gap or weakness targeted | Impact.
- Do not tell a small site to fight a dominant player on every head term; pick
  the niches it can win. Ignore vanity metrics.
