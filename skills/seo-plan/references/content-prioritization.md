# Content Audit, Prioritization and Topic Ideation

## Existing content audit (run before planning new content)

Inventory every indexable content URL and tag it:

| Status | Signal | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Strong | Comprehensive, ranks or earns traffic, current | Keep; use as a hub or link source |
| Thin | Shallow, outdated, no traffic | Expand or refresh, or merge |
| Overlapping | 2+ URLs on the same intent | Consolidate into one, 301 the rest |
| Gap | Topic the audience or competitors need, no URL | Create |

Quick wins from existing assets come first: refresh pages that nearly rank
(positions 4-20, see `seo-google/references/gsc-monthly-routine.md`),
consolidate overlaps, target low-competition keywords.

## Prioritization matrix

Score each idea 1-5 on search demand, ranking difficulty, business value (does
it attract the buyer?) and topical-authority contribution (does it strengthen a
cluster?).

| Quadrant | Profile | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Quick wins | Low difficulty + high business value | Do first |
| Big bets | High demand + high difficulty | Invest: best-quality pieces, link support |
| Fill-ins | Low demand + low difficulty | Batch later |
| Avoid | Low business value + high difficulty | Skip |

Rank by business value before raw volume; money pages (product, category,
service, pricing) before informational content. Size the plan to the team's
real capacity; never set a cadence the team cannot sustain at quality.

Priority queue: | # | Title | Keyword | Intent | Funnel stage | Quadrant | Est. words | New / Refresh / Merge |

## Topic ideation angles

Generate ideas across: how-to; what / why explainer; best-of; comparison and
alternatives; case study or original data; mistakes and myths; what's changing;
beginner's guide; advanced deep dive; templates, frameworks, checklists.

Per topic: working title (< 60 characters), primary keyword, intent, funnel
stage (TOFU / MOFU / BOFU), content type, difficulty (L / M / H), why it matters
to this audience, the angle that differs from what ranks now, 3-5 key sections.

Sequencing: pillar or foundation piece first, then low-difficulty quick wins,
then pieces that build on earlier ones. List the internal links each new topic
gives and receives.
