# Product Page Depth Modules and Editorial-Commerce Linking

## Content depth modules (in addition to the Product SEO Checklist)

- [ ] H1 = product name + form / variant + size or quantity (matches how people
      search and how AI answers name the item).
- [ ] Price and stock visible above the fold next to the primary CTA.
- [ ] Answer-first "What is [product]?" block, 40-60 words, self-contained
      (a quotable passage for snippets and AI answers).
- [ ] Specification table with the identifiers people and machines match on
      (model / MPN, GTIN, material or composition, dimensions, standards),
      mirrored in Product schema (`gtin*`, `mpn`, `additionalProperty`).
- [ ] "How it works / what's inside" section for technical products.
- [ ] Evidence: for technical, health-adjacent or regulated products, cite
      primary sources (standards bodies, peer-reviewed papers, test labs) in a
      consistent format; set a per-template minimum.
- [ ] Comparison table against 1-2 closely related products in your own catalog
      (captures "[A] vs [B]" queries; link both ways). Full comparison pages:
      `seo-competitor-pages` (catalog comparison pages).
- [ ] Quality / proof section linking to the central trust page.
- [ ] 5-7 product questions answered on-page in plain HTML.
- [ ] 3-4 related products.
- [ ] Links to the relevant buying guide, tool or calculator, and editorial
      article about this product.
- [ ] Meta description = product name + main differentiator; OG image = product image.
- [ ] Depth matches the catalog benchmark product (`store-template-checklists.md`).

## Editorial-commerce linking (both directions)

- Every product page links to its category, its buying guide, the most relevant
  editorial article, any tool or calculator, and the trust / proof page.
- Every article or guide that names a product links to that product (and its
  category) with descriptive anchor text.
- Category pages link to the guides that help choose within the category.
- Comparison pages link to both products; both products link back.
- Audit as a matrix: rows = products, columns = guide / article / tool / proof /
  comparison. Empty cells are tasks.
- Process rule: when an article is published, add links to it FROM the related
  product and category pages in the same release.
- Exception: editorial pages about items you must not sell (restricted or not
  stocked) do not link to product pages.
